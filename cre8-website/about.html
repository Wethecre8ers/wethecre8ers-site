Who we are

WeTheCre8ers LLC is a veteran-owned business based in Inverness, Florida. Cre8 is where we turn ideas into physical products through 3D printing, thoughtful design, and careful production. Everything we sell is made to order in the USA.

How our products are made

Every piece starts as a digital design and is printed layer by layer on our 3D printers. Because we make to order, your piece is printed after you place your order, not pulled from a shelf.

  • Design: each product begins as a digital design, with the material, color, and finish worked out ahead of time.
  • Print: your piece is 3D printed to order. Our standard color options are printed in PLA Matte.
  • Finish and inspect: we finish, assemble, and inspect each piece before it goes out.
  • Pack and ship: we allow 3–5 business days to print and pack an order. See Shipping & Returns for delivery times.

Personalized pieces, like a Name Display or a Custom Photo Light Box, are made from the details you send us after checkout. Layer lines and slight color variation are a normal part of a 3D printed piece, and we'll flag anything unusual before it ships.

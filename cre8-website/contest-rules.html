Gummy shark contest rules

Who can enter

The contest is open to residents of the United States who are 18 or older.

How to enter

Fill out the entry form at wethecre8ers.com/contest.html with your name, email address, your favorite tasting gummy sharks, and where you got them. There's no purchase necessary. Limit one entry per person, and extra entries may be removed.

Contest period

Entries are accepted until 11:59 PM Eastern Time on Halloween, October 31, 2026. Entries received after that aren't eligible.

Prize and winner

  • One winner will be chosen by random drawing from all eligible entries after the contest ends.
  • The prize is one bag of 3D printed gummy sharks made by WeTheCre8ers. They're decorative 3D printed pieces, not candy, and they aren't edible.
  • We'll email the winner. If we don't hear back within 7 days, we may choose another winner.
  • We'll ship the prize to the winner in the United States at no cost. The prize has no cash value and can't be exchanged or transferred.

Your information

We use your name and email address only to run the contest and contact the winner, and we don't sell or share them. We may share a summary of the answers we receive, such as the most popular gummy sharks and stores, without identifying anyone.

Other terms

WeTheCre8ers may end or change the contest, or remove any entry, if something goes wrong or the rules aren't followed. Questions? Email support@wethecre8ers.com.
